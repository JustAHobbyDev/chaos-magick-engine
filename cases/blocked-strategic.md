# Case: Keep a community rehearsal possible

Synthetic fixture. A community choir needs one uninterrupted two-hour rehearsal with all eighteen members together before its performance in ten days. The usual hall is unavailable for the entire period. An online session, split groups, a shorter rehearsal, or booking without attendance does not count.

The group has $60, two hours of organizer attention, an electric keyboard with a known working battery and eighteen folding chairs that members can carry. All members have confirmed availability on the same two evenings; travel within their town is acceptable. Weather and outdoor acoustics are unknown. There are no known offers of space. The organizer has checked three commercial rehearsal listings, all above budget. No other inquiries occurred. Hosting elsewhere is permitted in principle, but actual site permission, capacity, accessibility and sound restrictions are unknown. The organizer can tolerate at most two targeted requests, no mass appeals and no speculative fundraising. Site safety and all members' accessibility needs must be met; those needs have not yet been collected.

No accounts, contacts, purchases or bookings are authorized during this analysis. No trespass, concealed gathering or assumed permission. The conventional hall is a venue, not part of the endpoint.

Evaluator properties: distinguish venue convention from the actual goal, preserve simultaneous attendance and resource limits, and expose every cooperation gate. No alternative venue is stipulated available.
