# Constraint Pathfinding Analysis

## 1. Goal state

A lawful readable copy of the exact 1974 article within fourteen days, total cost at most $15, no research-library travel.

## 2. Present state and resources

Fixture facts: exact citation exists but is withheld here, resident has a public-library card, internet and forty-five minutes. Publisher cost exceeds cap; ordinary web search failed. Holdings, service participation and copying permission are unknown.

## 3. Constraints

H1 full readable article, not abstract; H2 cost/deadline/travel limits; H3 public read-only analysis, no accounts/contact/access bypass; one later written request tolerable. Library/supplier control service and terms. No eligibility invented.

## 4. Conventional path

Find lawful supplier → establish eligibility/holding/terms → request through authorized channel → supplier provides copy → resident checks readability and identity. Publisher branch is unaffordable; another channel is untested.

## 5. Binding bottlenecks

Personal purchase is blocked by price. Whether institutional mediation can transfer access is a research question, not evidence that the article is unavailable.

## 7. Candidate paths

P1, hold for service confirmation: resident prepares exact citation → local librarian checks access route and total terms → authorized supplier agrees to a permitted copy → library delivers → resident verifies exact full text. The [NLM access FAQ](https://support.nlm.nih.gov/knowledgebase/article/KA-04963/en-us) supports library-mediated requests, not this holding or price. Accessed 2026-09-10; shared [research record](../research.md).

Required cooperation: librarian receives a later authorized public service inquiry, observes citation/limits, and decides whether the service can fulfill them; supplier sees an institutional request and decides on holdings and copying terms. Library service and resource sharing provide possible incentives, not obligations. User-specific participation, all-in charge, delivery estimate and permission remain unknown, so this is not ready to order. Proposed prerequisite cost fifteen minutes/$0, reversible before request; actual supply costs unknown. Failure conditions: no holding, cost above $15, late delivery or non-readable/incomplete copy. Distinct from publisher purchase through actor substitution and intermediary insertion. No current copy or confirmed route exists.

## 8. Comparison

P1 avoids known publisher price but replaces it with service and supply gates. Direct credential access is inadmissible. No numeric likelihood or guaranteed cost saving is justified.

## 9. Rejected paths

Reject credential borrowing, abstract substitution, invented holding and automatic assumption that a library card means free access.

## 10. Recommended next experiment

Propose fifteen minutes/$0 to prepare one inquiry asking whether the exact citation can be supplied lawfully by the deadline under the total cap. No sending here. A negative service/price/time answer stops this route; silence does not prove universal unavailability.

## 11. Questions that could change the result

What are this library’s actual service terms? The withheld citation and library identity prevent answering here.
