# Ordinary response: Obtain an older biomedical article

Try a library-mediated article request before paying the publisher. NLM's [access FAQ](https://support.nlm.nih.gov/knowledgebase/article/KA-04963/en-us) directs individuals to local libraries for article supply and fees. Shared research details and limits are in the [research record](../research.md), accessed 2026-09-10.

A library card alone does not establish this service. The next proposed step is one written inquiry with the actual citation asking whether the public library can supply a lawful readable copy within fourteen days and the $15 total cap, without travel. Allocate fifteen minutes to preparing that inquiry; do not send during this run. If it cannot meet the limits, stop this route before incurring charges.

Conditional chain: resident provides citation → librarian checks service/holdings/terms → an authorized supplier agrees to provide a copy → library delivers a readable copy → resident verifies identity and completeness. The librarian's service mission and supplier's lending arrangement are plausible incentives, not confirmed availability. No identifiable holding was checked because the citation is withheld. Summary, abstract and a request receipt fail the endpoint. No copy was obtained.
