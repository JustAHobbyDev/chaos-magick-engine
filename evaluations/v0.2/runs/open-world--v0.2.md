## 5. Rival representations and discriminators

M1 personal-affiliation gate: predicts resident cannot request as institutional participant, explains failed direct approach but not whether a library can mediate. Opens authorized intermediary region.

M2 holding/terms gate: predicts even eligible mediation may fail to find or supply the article under C1. Explains publisher/web failure without proving affiliation is decisive. Opens holding/routing region.

Cheapest discriminator: actual library response to citation and caps. A service refusal distinguishes local eligibility from a supported service that cannot locate a holding; both can coexist. Official rules support role separation, not a winning model for this article.
