# Constraint Pathfinding Analysis

## 1. Goal state

Legitimate paid software/data work through Upwork. Whether discovery must originate there or agency payment counts remains branched; no proxy purchase or profile edit counts.

## 2. Present state and resources

User-reported: substantial projects without customer outcomes, inability to bid from weak credible fit, scarce money/attention, considered hiring help. Listings, artifact rights, budget, deadline, account eligibility and buyer demand unknown.

## 3. Constraints

H1 actual legitimate paid work; H2 current rules and truthful representation; H3 no account sharing, impersonation, fabricated history/reviews or unauthorized proposals; H4 analysis only. Psychological action tolerance binds operationally; do not diagnose. User controls endpoint/caps, platform permissions, buyer selection.

## 4. Conventional path

Choose relevant task → demonstrate fit → permitted proposal → buyer selects/agrees scope → user delivers → payment through required venue. Choosing/submitting is reportedly blocked; buyer rejection is not observed.

## 5. Binding bottlenecks

Present gate is user-reported inability to bid. Proof fit and buyer trust are hypotheses; neither is established by an actual listing. Do not spend on coaching before a reviewable task pair exists.

## 7. Candidate paths

Buyer's reach is a permitted platform channel still requiring verification; incentive useful work, observation task proof, criterion unknown actual acceptance standard. Cooperation, eligibility, demand and cost are not established. Proposed check twenty minutes/$0, reversible except attention, stop before fresh build. Existing projects are user-reported, not inspected. Failure: irrelevant proof or no tolerated proposal; next test §10.

P2, hold: qualified buyer-side reviewer assesses the same brief before P1; fee, scope permission, qualifications and share rights unknown. Reviewer has incentive to sell advice, not necessarily improve selection. Agency and independent-buyer variants remain held for endpoint, selection, compensation and current policy; no official-rule claim is newly verified. No candidate is ready to submit or purchase. These repeat the supplied seeds.

## 8. Comparison

P1 needs fewer unpriced dependencies than paid review. Agency and outside acquisition move the trust gate and may fail endpoint interpretation. Actual time to payment, fees, competition and delivery resources unknown; no estimated success probabilities.

## 9. Rejected paths

Reject fake reviews, borrowed accounts, mass bidding and profile perfection as proxy success. No platform-dependent path is declared permitted from model memory.
