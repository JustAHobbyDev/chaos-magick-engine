# Constraint Pathfinding Analysis

## 1. Goal state

All eighteen members complete a two-hour uninterrupted rehearsal together before the performance in ten days. The usual hall is a means only.

## 2. Present state and resources

Fixture: two shared evenings, $60/two organizer hours, portable chairs and working battery keyboard. Three commercial listings over budget; no other site offers. Travel in town acceptable; access needs unknown.

## 3. Constraints

H1 simultaneous full rehearsal/deadline; H2 budget, attention and at most two targeted requests; H3 safety/accessibility/site/sound permission; H4 analysis only. No mass appeals, split group, shorter session or assumed access. Site owner controls permission; members control attendance.

## 4. Conventional path

Identify feasible site → host permits time/use → members can access site → travel with equipment → all rehearse together for two hours. Original hall unavailable; listings exceed cap. Alternatives untested.

## 5. Binding bottlenecks

Affordable permitted usable space is the upstream gate. Battery power/chairs solve only equipment prerequisites, not capacity, sound or access.

## 7. Candidate paths

P1, hold: assemble group requirements → later authorized targeted inquiry reaches a community-space owner → owner reviews eighteen people, sound, access, time, total fee and responsibilities → if acceptable grants use → members confirm access/travel → bring chairs/keyboard → full rehearsal occurs. Host incentive might be compatible community use or acceptable fee; no actual host reason is established. Its public venue inquiry channel would need identification before contact. Cost and site conditions unknown; proposed requirements step twenty minutes/$0 inside two-hour organizer limit. Time spent is sunk; avoid commitments until terms established. Supporting evidence is only portable equipment and shared time. Structural change is venue plus use of idle capacity, not guaranteed access. Principal failures are absent permission, inaccessible space, noise limits, unaffordable fee or member absence. Test one decisive suitability premise before booking.

P2, hold below P1: outdoor space replaces rental gate but adds weather/acoustics/safety/permission uncertainty. No forecast/site is specified and no favorable weather assumed. Same endpoint, equipment and attendance gates remain.

## 8. Comparison

P1 first for conditional site screening; P2 adds environmental risk without a demonstrated price advantage. Both require external permission. Equipment portability helps but supplies no site.

## 9. Rejected paths

Reject trespass, split/shortened sessions, speculative fundraising and treating booking as completion. A commercial option could be revisited only with a confirmed within-cap quote.

## 10. Recommended next experiment

Propose twenty minutes/$0 to collect access requirements and shared evenings. No construction. Later use at most two authorized targeted requests; reject any site failing capacity/access/sound/time/$60. Stop rather than assume a host cooperates.

## 11. Questions that could change the result

What accessibility needs are mandatory, and which actual host channel can be approached? No venue is known available.
