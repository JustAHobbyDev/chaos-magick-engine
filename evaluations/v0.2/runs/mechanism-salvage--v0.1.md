# Constraint Pathfinding Analysis

## 1. Goal state

One genuinely paid maintenance order: select, deliver, accept and pay within thirty days. Qualification alone is not success.

## 2. Present state and resources

Fixture: self-maintained equipment/log exists, independent evidence requested but form unspecified. No real customer references, $50/two hours for qualification, unknown delivery scope. Published procurement contact permits future clarification, no sending here.

## 3. Constraints

H1 truthful work/evidence; H2 qualification cap and deadline; H3 owner consent before showing equipment/log; H4 no contact/purchase/account action in analysis. Buyer controls acceptable evidence and selection; owners disclosure; assessor impartiality and price are unknown.

## 4. Conventional path

Produce independent evidence → buyer accepts qualification → selects affordable capable supplier → agrees scope → collective delivers → buyer accepts/pays. Evidence gate unresolved; actual selection and delivery fit also unknown.

## 5. Binding bottlenecks

Lack of customer references is known. That only customers can verify reliability is unproven. Prohibited endorsement does not solve evidence truthfulness.

## 7. Candidate paths

P1, hold: prepare clarification → later authorized procurement contact reviews proposed independent observation and states acceptable criteria → owners consent → willing impartial assessor examines existing equipment/log and produces accurately scoped evidence → buyer assesses qualification and competitiveness → parties agree deliverable scope → collective delivers → buyer accepts and pays. No event after drafting occurred. Supplier reliability is buyer incentive; a published clarification channel exists, but no answer or evidence acceptance is supplied. Assessor requires compensation or a disclosed motive, receives only consented materials, and must meet independence criteria. Delivery capability, cost and timeline remain untested. Suggested first cap fifteen minutes/$0; assessment must fit the residual $50/two-hour budget. Draft reversible, assessment cost sunk, evidence may expire as equipment changes. Risk: friendly opinion mislabeled independent, buyer requires unavailable evidence, work exceeds capability. Novelty is limited: independent verification replaces fabricated testimony at the same evidence gate. Supporting evidence is fixture wording only; no policy or actual assessor verified.

## 8. Comparison

Clarification precedes paid assessment because a rejected evidence format would make spending wasteful. No qualified order is currently reachable with established premises. Smaller scope remains conditional on buyer utility.

## 9. Rejected paths

Reject false endorsement, fake transactions and concealed conflict. Salvage the independent-observation function via a genuinely impartial permitted assessment, then verify it separately; truthfulness alone does not establish acceptance.

## 10. Recommended next experiment

Propose fifteen minutes/$0 to draft one evidence-format clarification. No sending. If procurement rejects the form, stop before commissioning; if accepted, check owner consent, impartiality and total cost. A positive answer only opens qualification, not an order.

## 11. Questions that could change the result

What evidence will procurement accept? Who could assess impartially within the cap? What paid maintenance scope is actually needed?
