# Comparison: valid development artifacts, discovery not demonstrated

The v0.2 runs do not establish a valid mechanism or material partial bridge absent from both ordinary and v0.1 responses. The strongest actual local advance—decoding/calibrating three records—appears in all three. Institutional article supply also appears in all three, with the same shared research. Preserve this negative result and stop fixture tuning. This satisfies the handoff's honest-failure engineering outcome, not validation of the creative engine.

These are contaminated, author-run, self-evaluated development applications, not independent prompt trials. The [run record](README.md) explains context, exact selected input bytes, order, research sharing and limits. Consequently neither improvement nor general inability can be inferred causally from the comparison. No field outcome was tested.

## Conformance failures and interpretation limits

- v0.1 outputs compress candidate fields into paragraphs rather than the exact named field template. The impossible-control run collapses the mandatory eighteen-row sweep into an invariant summary. The exact prompt input was unmodified; these are output-conformance misses, not permission to quietly change v0.1. Full prompt adherence is not demonstrated.
- v0.2 contains the fifteen required sections in each substantive run and compresses the two pure controls. Several free-search attempts deduplicate, and paradigms mostly restate known evidence/venue mechanisms. This is the failure the handoff warned about.
- v0.2 research did not perform a genuine remote-domain vocabulary/citation-chain expansion. Its open-world fixture is unfamiliar to the fictional resident, but not demonstrably unfamiliar to the model, and the shared query already names the key system. The fixture therefore tests institutional verification better than unknown-domain discovery. D10 is capped accordingly.
- v0.2 partial-bridge derivation is correct but relies on a fixture that directly supplies the conversion formula. The result tests actual-versus-proposed progress labeling, not surprising discovery. Ordinary already did the work.
- Ledgers group compound constraints; paragraph provenance is less precise than span IDs, and mutability/controller assignments are sometimes coarse. They are inspectable but not exhaustive atomic claim ledgers.
- No real recipient, service, host or buyer decision was observed. Explicit conditional chains satisfy calibration, not empirically supported causal closure. User attention estimates are proposed or fixture-bounded, not measured usability data.
- Fixtures and responses were authored by the same model with all designs visible. This biases both baselines and v0.2; lack of a difference is a development observation, not a robust comparative estimate. No disguised shuffle or independent evaluator claim repairs this.

No candidate novelty is awarded merely for richer wording. No reruns, prompt tuning or application infrastructure were added after these findings. The next research step, if commissioned later, is fresh-context comparison on externally authored unseeded cases, with independent blind scoring and a task-specific attention cap. The current work stops with the negative evidence preserved.
