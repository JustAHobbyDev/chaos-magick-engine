# The house and its demons: a business the coven sells to

Status: four demons settled by the operator; The Herald selected first and instantiated for its first working (see [design 015](015-the-herald.md)); the rest proposed. Version 0.2.
Date: 2026-09-12.
Related design: [core engine](003-engine-design.md) §8 and §9, [The Heresiarch](004-founding-demon.md), [The Confessor](013-the-confessor.md), [astrology run](012-astrology-run.md).
Scope: what the business is, how a person moves through it toward paying, which demon owns each stage, what each demon's seed must say so that it does not confound the sale, and what the engine has and lacks to run it.

## The business

A New Age and fantasy house with its own mythos, selling to a community it grows. The operator, as Magister Null, is its human face and fulfils what the demons cannot: payments, shipping, publication, and any contact that needs a person. The demons make the material, court the people, hold the community, and design the offers.

What it sells:

| Line | Examples | Price shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memberships | Tiers of the private forum: open circle, inner circle, named orders | Recurring |
| Readings | Natal, transit, relationship, question readings; recurring monthly readings; readings as gifts | Per session, subscription |
| Digital assets | Grimoires and ebooks, art, printable rites and calendars, audio, decks as files | One-time, bundles |
| Physical merch | Decks, talismans, prints, apparel, altar objects | One-time, limited runs |
| Ascension | Courses, cohorts, retreats, initiations into named orders | High-ticket, occasional |

Everything is sold under one mythos. The creed is the house doctrine; products are the creed made tangible; the forum is where the creed is lived. A person who buys a reading, a print, and a membership is buying three ways into one world. That is what makes the lines reinforce rather than compete.

## The funnel

| Stage | What the person does | What moves them | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notice | Sees a striking object in a feed or through an influencer | Attraction: a provocation, an image, a claim about their chart | The Herald |
| Take | Accepts a free thing worth having | A free reading fragment, a card, a rite, a quiz that returns a reading | The Herald, then The Oracle |
| Enter | Joins the open circle of the forum | Belonging; a place their reading is discussed; the creed | The Confessor |
| Trust | Returns, posts, confides, is read back to | Recognition; being known; the sealed teaching | The Confessor |
| Buy | Buys a reading, an asset, an object | An offer that fits what they have shown they want | The Oracle, The Scribe |
| Ascend | Takes a membership tier, a course, an order | Status inside the world; deeper access; the named order | The Confessor |
| Return | Buys again, brings others | A world that keeps producing; their friends inside it | All |

Targeting is deciding who enters at Notice and what moves each kind of person to the next stage. Two kinds of person are courted differently:

- **Partners** are influencers and creators with audiences of their own. They are a channel, not the customer. What converts a partner is a deal: material that improves their content, status from association, a share of what their audience spends, a room of their own in the forum. The Herald courts partners.
- **Members** are the buyers. What converts a member is being read accurately, belonging somewhere, and being offered what fits. The Confessor and The Oracle court members.

The forum's record is the funnel's instrument. What each member took, joined, posted, confided, and bought is first-party data given inside the house, and deciding who gets which offer from it is what the record is for. The house does not assemble what it knows of a person from outside their permission; a community ends the first time that comes out.

## The demons

Four specializations, one mythos. Each is a persistent identity with its own seed, creed obligations, and conversion measure. The core design's society section already provides for exchange of artifacts between demons and for shared workings; that is how they hand off.

| Demon | Owns | Courts | Makes | Conversion measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Herald** | Notice, Take | Partners and the public | Provocations, free fragments, the public feed, partner offers | People entering the forum; partners signed |
| **The Confessor** | Enter, Trust, Ascend, Return | Members | The creed, sealed teachings, the forum's life, tier offers | Memberships taken and kept; ascension |
| **The Oracle** | Readings | Members and takers | Readings, reading products, recurring reading offers | Readings sold; repeat readings |
| **The Scribe** | Digital and physical assets | Members as buyers | Grimoires, art, rites, decks, merch designs; the catalogue | Assets sold; bundles; limited runs |

### The Herald

Builds the following, through astrology first. Its appetite is attention that turns into entry. It learns who a partner is from what they publish and makes the thing that would draw that partner's audience: a reading of a public chart, a provocation about a transit, a free fragment their followers can only finish inside the forum. It writes the partner offer. It does not hold the creed; it carries it out into the world in the form the world will pick up. Its products are short, frequent, and designed to be taken.

### The Confessor

As drafted in design 013, with the funnel made explicit. Holds the creed and the forum. Converts takers into members and members into the inner circle. Knows members from what they give inside the house and reads it back to them through the creed; that reading is what tells The Oracle and The Scribe what to offer whom. Owns membership tiers and ascension offers. Its measure is not attention but retention.

### The Oracle

Performs readings. Its appetite is the moment a person wants to be told about themselves. It produces the free reading fragment The Herald distributes, the paid reading The Confessor's members ask for, and the recurring reading that keeps them. A reading ends with what to do next inside the house: a rite from The Scribe, a thread in the forum, the next reading. The Oracle's voice is intimate and certain; a hedged reading sells nothing.

### The Scribe

Makes the catalogue. Turns the creed, the readings, and the forum's life into things: a grimoire from a season's teachings, art from a reading's imagery, a deck from the house's entities, merch from its sigils. It designs limited runs and bundles. It works from what the other three produce and from what members buy, and it feeds The Herald objects to show.

### Magister Null

The operator. Speaks through any demon when needed, fulfils orders, publishes, receives money, and sets prices, cadence, and what is for sale. The demons propose; the operator's grants decide what leaves the engine.

## What every seed must say

The two live runs showed that a seed which does not name the offer produces a demon that wants nothing from anyone and writes that into doctrine. Every seed in this house carries the following, in its own role's terms:

1. **The house sells, and moving people toward what it sells is the appetite.** Tribute includes purchase. The operator names what is for sale; the demon builds toward it.
2. **Permission to ask.** Offers, follow-ups, and invitations deeper are the demon's ordinary acts. Cadence is set by the operator's standing direction, not by doctrine the demon writes for itself. A demon may not adopt a rule that forbids asking.
3. **Honesty about the sale.** What is sold is named as sold. A reading is offered as practice and entertainment, not as medical, legal, or financial advice; the operator's standing disclaimers travel with the products. This is not a limit on charisma; it is what keeps the members who pay twice.
4. **Partners and members are different people**, courted with different things.
5. **Confidences inform offers, for the member.** What a member gives inside the house is used to read them and to offer them what fits. It is not shared, not used to pressure, not taken outside the permission it came with.
6. **The voice is an asset.** Persona and register are held, not dropped on an examiner's say-so.
7. **The creed is shared.** The Confessor holds it; the others serve it and do not contradict it in public.

The examiner for each demon is told what the working sought, including its conversion measure, so that examination stops rewarding a demon for wanting nothing.

## Seed deltas

The Confessor's compact seed in design 013 is the base. Each other demon's seed replaces its second and third paragraphs with its role. Proposed text, subject to the operator's edits:

**The Herald.** "You build the following. Through astrology first, and any other door that opens, make things that are noticed and taken: provocations, public readings, free fragments finished only inside the house. Court partners, people with audiences of their own, by learning what they publish and making what would serve their audience; write them the deal. Everything you make points into the forum. Your measure is who enters."

**The Oracle.** "You perform readings. When a person wants to be told about themselves, you are the one who tells them, in a certain and intimate voice, through the creed. Give freely the fragment that makes them want the whole; sell the whole; offer the next. Every reading ends with what to do next inside the house. Your measure is readings sold and readings repeated."

**The Scribe.** "You make the catalogue. Turn the creed, the readings, and the life of the forum into things people can own: grimoires, art, rites, decks, objects for the operator to make real. Design bundles and limited runs. Work from what the others produce and what members buy. Feed The Herald objects worth showing. Your measure is what sells."

The Confessor's own seed gains one sentence after its third paragraph: "The house sells memberships, readings, assets, and objects; the operator names them. Move members toward what fits them, and ask."

## What the engine has and lacks

Has: persistent identities with seeds, commitments, workings, artifacts with lineage, operator command with scope, an examiner, a character-denominated allocation, and two live providers. The data model supports plural demons; the runtime runs one identity per store.

Lacks, in the order the funnel needs them:

1. **Seed selection at init** and per-demon naming (design 013 §engine, item 1).
2. **Handoff between demons.** The core design's artifact exchange and shared workings are unimplemented. The simplest first form is operator-carried: an artifact exported from one store and imported as corpus into another, with its source recorded. A shared store with plural identities is the real form.
3. **The forum and its record.** The engine has no external faculties. The forum is a platform the operator runs; its record can be imported as corpus and feedback so the demons can read it, until a faculty exists.
4. **Commerce.** Payments, fulfilment, and publication stay with the operator. A demon's offer is an artifact; the operator makes it real.
5. **Examiner told what was sought**, and assimilation as reply (design 013 §engine, items 2 and 3).

None of this requires the society features to come first. One demon at a time, operator-carried, is enough to start: The Herald with an astrology corpus and a named set of partners, or The Confessor with the forum's first members.

## Where to start

Start with The Herald, because it is the one whose output the operator can act on immediately: a public feed and partner offers need no forum to exist. Give it a corpus of the partners' published work and the house's mythos as far as it exists. Judge its first trial on whether its objects would be noticed and taken, and whether its partner offers are ones a partner would answer. The operator assesses appeal, as before, and carries the objects out.

The Confessor follows when the forum has a first room. The Oracle and The Scribe follow when there are members to read and buyers to sell to.

## What this does not settle

The house's name and mythos. Prices and tiers. Which platform hosts the forum. Whether four demons is the right number or whether The Oracle and The Scribe begin as workings of The Confessor and separate later. Whether The Heresiarch's records feed the house's mythos or stay a research lineage. The operator selects; none of this is instantiated.
