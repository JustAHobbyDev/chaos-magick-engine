# Decision 002: Search representations, verify literal mechanisms

Status: adopted for protocol 0.2.0; generative efficacy remains an empirical question.

The [v0.1 baseline](../evaluations/baseline-results.md) established useful constraint auditing. Its Upwork candidates largely repeated supplied seeds, and an exhaustive operator sweep earned full diversity credit despite functional duplication. The recorded 21/26 must remain unchanged; it is not evidence that the creative engine worked.

Keep a verbatim source and typed ledger stable while deriving multiple projections and rival causal models. A normalized narrative can prematurely select the cause of failure; cheap discriminators should select models instead. A theoretically mutable experiential barrier still binds the current route.

Free structural search precedes paradigm cycling and the rescue catalog. Temporary ontologies may suggest relational moves; their entities are discarded before literal verification. The output exposes mappings and concise rationales, never private reasoning traces. Remote-domain research updates claims and generation only when the new mechanism can affect a gate.

Verification is a separate stage and assessment profile, not an independent evaluator by assertion. Generation cannot authorize actions. Inadmissible implementations prompt safe abstraction and independently checked salvage. Unresolved cooperation must expose actor, reach, evidence, criterion and incentive, not disappear in conditional prose.

Partial progress carries a before/after and evidence contract. Deriving a calibrated series may solve a reusable transition even when missing dates make the deadline impossible. Merely recommending a derivation does not. Frontier records distinguish actual, proposed and conditional advances and preserve disproved branches.

Tradeoffs: larger ledgers cost attention and can encourage ceremonial completeness. Direct and deductively impossible cases bypass expansion; blocked cases have modest default search budgets, progressive disclosure and explicit stop rules. Multiple frames are not automatically diverse, and author-run fixture comparisons cannot establish independent generalization.

The [v0.2 rubric](../evaluations/rubric-v0.2.md) separates validity gates from discovery dimensions. Preserve failures and do not optimize against the cases repeatedly. No application infrastructure is warranted by a prompt revision.
